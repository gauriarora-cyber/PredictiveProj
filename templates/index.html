KEY RESEARCH INSIGHT

Alcohol is the strongest predictor in the linear model.

Volatile acidity and sulphates also contribute strongly. The low recall for high-quality wines highlights the challenge of class imbalance.

BEHIND THE PREDICTION

Methodology

01

Data preparation

Load the UCI red wine dataset, inspect missing values and remove duplicate records.

02

Feature scaling

Split the data into training and test sets, then standardize the 11 input features.

03

Model training

Train Linear Regression for numerical scores and Logistic Regression for two quality classes.

04

Evaluation & inference

Evaluate on held-out data and use the fitted models to predict new wine samples.
